Book Recommendation & Prediction

Modeling what readers might pick up next — and how they might rate it.

Recommendation Systems
UC San Diego · CSE 158
PythonCollaborative FilteringJaccard SimilarityRating PredictionLatent Factors

Two prediction tasks

The project considers two different questions: whether a user will read a book and what rating they might give it. Both require extracting signals from observed interactions without assuming every user or title has rich history.

How the models work

01
Read prediction

Started with popularity-based heuristics and examined user–book signals, including Jaccard similarity between audiences of different books.

02
Rating prediction

Modeled the global mean together with regularized user and item bias terms. The accompanying project writeup also explores a latent-factor matrix factorization approach.

03
Cold-start handling

Used user averages, item averages, or the global average to produce fallback rating predictions when information is missing.

What I learned

Recommendation systems must balance personalization against sparse interaction histories. The project compares simpler, more interpretable baselines with richer collaborative signals; the materials provided do not establish a reliable headline evaluation score, so no accuracy claim is shown here.
